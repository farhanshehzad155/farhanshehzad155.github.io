### The chain

Keyword research, topic generation, drafting, image generation, internal linking, publishing.
Each stage hands structured output to the next rather than free text, so a failure is
attributable to a stage instead of surfacing as a bad article at the end.

### The part that is not the model

FR-CP2 exists because a reader in 2026 has seen a lot of AI content pipelines and assumes the
worst. Fair enough. So this section has to be specific rather than reassuring.

Internal linking is the clearest example. Asking a model to add internal links produces links
that look right and point nowhere, because the model is predicting plausible URLs rather than
consulting the site. The pipeline instead keeps its own index of pages that exist and links
against that, so a link either resolves or is not inserted.
